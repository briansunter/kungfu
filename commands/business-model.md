# Business Model Command

Generate a comprehensive Business Model Canvas and supporting documentation for
business ideas, products, or services.

## Instructions

1. **Gather Information**: Ask the user for:
   - Product or service description
   - Target market or customer segment
   - Value proposition (what problem does it solve?)
   - Current stage (idea, MVP, scaling, established)
   - Industry or sector

2. **Generate Business Model Canvas**:

   **Customer Segments**:
   - Who are the target customers?
   - Create user personas if helpful
   - Segment by demographics, behaviors, needs

   **Value Propositions**:
   - What value is delivered to customers?
   - Which problems are being solved?
   - Unique selling points and differentiation

   **Channels**:
   - How do you reach customers?
   - Distribution methods
   - Marketing and sales channels
   - Customer touchpoints

   **Customer Relationships**:
   - Type of relationship (personal, automated, community)
   - Customer acquisition and retention strategies
   - Support and success models

   **Revenue Streams**:
   - How does the business generate revenue?
   - Pricing models (subscription, one-time, freemium, etc.)
   - Revenue sources and margins

   **Key Resources**:
   - Essential resources required
   - Physical, intellectual, human, and financial resources
   - Partnerships and dependencies

   **Key Activities**:
   - Core operations and processes
   - Production, problem-solving, platform/network activities
   - Critical success factors

   **Key Partnerships**:
   - Suppliers and partners
   - Strategic alliances
   - Joint ventures

   **Cost Structure**:
   - Fixed and variable costs
   - Economies of scale
   - Cost drivers and optimization opportunities

3. **Provide Additional Analysis**:
   - Validation questions and assumptions
   - Risk factors and mitigation strategies
   - Growth and scaling considerations
   - Competitive landscape overview

4. **Suggest Next Steps**:
   - MVP prioritization
   - Testing and validation approach
   - Go-to-market strategy
   - Key metrics to track

## Output Format

Present the business model in:

- Visual Business Model Canvas (9-box layout in text/markdown)
- Detailed explanations for each section
- Strategic recommendations
- Action items with priorities
