# Indie SaaS Roadmap Generator

This command generates a comprehensive 18-month roadmap for building a solo SaaS
business, based on the 4-phase journey framework from indie SaaS research.

## What This Does

Generates a complete roadmap covering:

- **Phase 1**: Validation (Months 0-2) - Prove demand before building
- **Phase 2**: MVP Launch (Months 2-6) - Launch to early adopters
- **Phase 3**: Growth (Months 6-18) - Reach sustainable $10K MRR
- **Phase 4**: Scaling Decision (18+ months) - Choose lifestyle vs. hypergrowth

## Using This Command

Provide context about your SaaS idea, and this command will generate a
customized roadmap.

**Input: Tell Claude about:**

- Your SaaS idea or concept
- Current stage (idea stage, validating, building, etc.)
- Target market (B2B, B2C, niche, etc.)
- Technical background (developer, non-technical, mixed)
- Time availability (hours/week)
- Financial runway

**Output: Customized roadmap including:**

- Phase-by-phase breakdown
- Specific tactics for each phase
- Success metrics to hit
- Time investment expectations
- Potential risks and mitigation strategies
- Related skills to use for each phase

## The 4-Phase Framework

### Phase 1: Validation (Months 0-2)

**Goal**: Prove demand exists before building

**Key Tactics:**

- Fake door landing pages
- Pre-selling (get 10 pre-orders)
- No-code MVP validation
- Community immersion (Indie Hackers, Reddit)
- Competitor analysis (3-star reviews reveal unmet needs)

**Success Metric**: 10 pre-orders or 100 validated emails

**Time Investment**: 10-20 hours/week

**Related Skills:**

- `indie-saas-validation-master` - Complete validation framework
- `community-growth-specialist` - Community immersion tactics

**Red Flags:**

- Can't get 10 people to join waitlist
- No one willing to pre-pay even with discount
- Competitors already solving this well
- Problem not urgent enough

---

### Phase 2: MVP Launch (Months 2-6)

**Goal**: Launch product to early adopters

**Key Tactics:**

- Build MVP in 4 weeks (not 6 months)
- Start community engagement (Indie Hackers, Reddit)
- Implement tiered pricing ($29/$99/$199)
- Launch on multiple platforms (Indie Hackers > Product Hunt)
- Focus intensely on first 10 customers

**Success Metric**: $1K-$3K MRR

**Time Investment**: 20-40 hours/week

**Related Skills:**

- `pricing-strategy-designer` - Tiered pricing setup
- `technical-automation-architect` - Boring stack selection
- `community-growth-specialist` - Launch day tactics

**Red Flags:**

- Taking 3+ months to build MVP
- No one willing to pay after launch
- Building features no one asked for
- Ignoring early customer feedback

---

### Phase 3: Growth (Months 6-18)

**Goal**: Reach sustainable $10K MRR

**Key Tactics:**

- Content marketing flywheel (90/10 rule)
- Programmatic SEO (if applicable)
- Referral program (K-factor optimization)
- Onboarding optimization (<7 day TTV)
- Churn reduction (<1% monthly)
- Build in public consistently

**Success Metric**: $10K MRR with <5% monthly churn

**Time Investment**: 30-50 hours/week

**Related Skills:**

- `community-growth-specialist` - Content and community
- `customer-retention-optimizer` - Churn reduction
- `solo-operations-manager` - Weekly operational rhythms

**Red Flags:**

- Stuck at $3K MRR for 3+ months
- Monthly churn >5%
- No time for strategic work (firefighting all day)
- Early customers churning fast

---

### Phase 4: Scaling Decision (Months 18+)

**Goal**: Choose your path: Lifestyle vs. Hypergrowth

**The Decision Matrix:**

**Path A: Lifestyle Business** ✨

- Optimize for freedom, not maximal growth
- 80%+ profit margins
- Stay solo or hire 1-2 contractors
- Work 20-30 hours/week
- $30K-$100K MRR ceiling typical
- Focus on profitability over growth

**Path B: Hypergrowth Scale** 🚀

- Optimize for growth, reinvest profits
- Hire at $100K ARR (after systemizing)
- Build team and processes
- Work 40-60 hours/week
- $100K+ ARR with team
- Focus on market share

**Success Metric**: $30K-$100K MRR (lifestyle) or $100K+ ARR with team (scale)

**Time Investment**: Varies by choice

**Related Skills:**

- `systemization-documentation-expert` - Document before hiring
- `solo-operations-manager` - Optimize solo operations
- `customer-retention-optimizer` - Scale retention systems

**Decision Framework:**

- Do you want to be a manager or builder?
- What's your desired income ceiling?
- How important is time freedom?
- Are you excited about hiring or dread it?

## Timeline Expectations

### Realistic Timeline

**Month 0-2**: Validation

- Expected outcome: Proof of demand (pre-orders or waitlist)
- Don't move forward until validation achieved

**Month 2-6**: MVP and Early Customers

- Expected outcome: $1K-$3K MRR, 10-20 paying customers
- Many get stuck here - push through to $10K

**Month 6-12**: The Grind

- Expected outcome: $5K-$10K MRR, improving unit economics
- Hardest phase - burnout risk high
- Use solo-operations-manager to survive

**Month 12-18**: Growth Engine

- Expected outcome: $10K MRR, sustainable churn
- Growth systems in place
- Ready to decide on scaling path

**Month 18+**: Decision Point

- Expected outcome: Choose lifestyle vs. scale
- If lifestyle: Optimize for freedom and margins
- If scale: Systemize and hire (systemization-documentation-expert)

### Common Timeline Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Rushing Validation**

- Spending 2 weeks validating instead of 2 months
- Moving to building too soon
- Result: Building something no one wants

**Mistake 2: Perfectionist MVP**

- Taking 6 months to build MVP instead of 4 weeks
- Over-building with unnecessary features
- Result: Lost momentum, wasted time

**Mistake 3: Giving Up at $3K MRR**

- Getting stuck in early traction plateau
- Not pushing through to $10K MRR
- Result: Never reaching escape velocity

**Mistake 4: Hiring Too Soon**

- Hiring at $30K ARR before systemizing
- Founder dependency increases instead of decreases
- Result: Chaos, not scalability

## Risk Mitigation by Phase

### Phase 1 Risks

**Risk**: Building something no one wants

- **Mitigation**: 10 pre-orders before writing code
- **Mitigation**: Talk to 50 potential customers
- **Mitigation**: Validate with no-code tools first

### Phase 2 Risks

**Risk**: Building too slowly

- **Mitigation**: 4-week MVP deadline
- **Mitigation**: Use boring tech stack
- **Mitigation**: Cut scope ruthlessly

### Phase 3 Risks

**Risk**: Burnout from grinding

- **Mitigation**: Weekly operational rhythms
- **Mitigation**: Protect personal boundaries
- **Mitigation**: Focus on high-impact activities only

### Phase 4 Risks

**Risk**: Wrong scaling choice

- **Mitigation**: Honest self-assessment of goals
- **Mitigation**: Don't scale just because others do
- **Mitigation**: Lifestyle is valid success
