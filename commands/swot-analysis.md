# SWOT Analysis Command

Generate a comprehensive SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats)
analysis for the specified business, product, or project.

## Instructions

1. **Understand the Context**: Ask the user for clarification on:
   - What is being analyzed (company, product, project, idea)
   - Industry or market sector
   - Target audience or market
   - Geographic scope (local, regional, global)

2. **Research the Subject** (if needed):
   - Search for relevant market information
   - Identify competitors and industry trends
   - Analyze market conditions

3. **Generate SWOT Analysis**:

   **Strengths (Internal, Positive)**:
   - Core competencies and advantages
   - Unique selling propositions
   - Resources and capabilities
   - Market position and brand strength
   - Team expertise

   **Weaknesses (Internal, Negative)**:
   - Limitations and gaps
   - Resource constraints
   - Areas for improvement
   - Competitive disadvantages
   - Operational challenges

   **Opportunities (External, Positive)**:
   - Market trends and growth areas
   - Untapped customer segments
   - Technological advancements
   - Strategic partnerships
   - Market gaps

   **Threats (External, Negative)**:
   - Competitive pressures
   - Market risks and disruptions
   - Regulatory changes
   - Economic factors
   - Technological obsolescence

4. **Provide Strategic Recommendations**:
   - Leverage strengths to capitalize on opportunities
   - Address weaknesses to mitigate threats
   - Prioritize actions based on impact
   - Suggest next steps and timelines

## Output Format

Present the analysis in a clear, structured format with:

- Bullet points for each SWOT category
- Brief explanations for each point
- Strategic recommendations section
- Priority action items

## Example

```text
User: /swot-analysis for a local coffee shop

Claude will generate:
- Strengths: Prime location, loyal customer base, quality products
- Weaknesses: Limited seating, higher prices, no delivery service
- Opportunities: Catering services, online ordering, expansion
- Threats: Chain competition, rising costs, changing preferences
- Recommendations: Focus on catering, implement loyalty program, add delivery
```
