**Source**: Report 2: GTM, Marketing & Customer Acquisition (engagement strategies)
**Confidence**: HIGH - Proven 4-month framework with real results

## The 90/10 Rule Explained

**90% Value, 10% Promotion**

What this means in practice:
- For every 10 helpful comments you write → mention your product once
- For every 100 posts that provide value → 10 posts mention your work
- For every 90 minutes helping others → 10 minutes sharing your journey

**Why 90/10 matters**:
- Self-promotional posts get 0-3 comments and die
- Value-focused posts get 30-50+ comments and convert
- Community can smell marketing from miles away
- Trust is earned through consistent value, not pitches

**Wrong ratios** (don't do this):
- ❌ 70/30: Seen as self-promotional, community disengages
- ❌ 50/50: Ignored or downvoted, reputation damaged
- ❌ 30/70: Banned from many communities

**Right ratio**:
- ✅ 90/10: Respected, trusted, converts better

## 4-Month Content Calendar

### Month 1: Foundation (Zero Self-Promotion)

**Goal**: Learn community culture, build reputation

**Week 1: Observe**
- Read top 20 posts of all time in each target community
- Identify common pain points
- Note language and terminology
- Understand what gets upvoted vs ignored
- **Action**: 0 posts, just reading

**Week 2-3: Start Helping**
- Answer 3-5 questions daily with detailed, helpful responses
- Share insights from your experience when relevant
- Be genuinely useful, not "helpful to get something"
- **Action**: 75-100 comments, 0 self-promotion

**Week 4: Establish Presence**
- Continue daily engagement (5-10 comments/day)
- Write 1-2 posts sharing lessons learned (no product mention)
- Ask genuine questions about the community's challenges
- **Action**: 100-140 comments, 2 helpful posts

**Month 1 Metrics**:
- Comments written: 150-250
- Posts created: 2-3
- Mentions of own work: 0
- Goal: Become known as helpful community member

---

### Month 2: First Journey Post

**Goal**: Share your journey, start transparent storytelling

**Week 5: First Journey Post**
- Post template: "I'm building X to solve Y. Here's my progress."
- Include: Problem you're solving, why it matters, your approach
- Ask for specific feedback
- Respond to every comment within minutes
- **Action**: 1 journey post, 35-50 comments

**Week 6-7: Continue Value-First**
- Daily comments: 5-10/day helping others
- Share 1 tactical insight: "Here's how I approached X"
- Reference your journey when relevant: "When I was building..."
- **Action**: 70-100 comments, 1 tactical post

**Week 8: Milestone Update**
- Post: "One month of building [product]. Here's what I learned."
- Share specific numbers (even if small): "127 emails collected"
- Be honest about setbacks
- Ask: "What would you do differently?"
- **Action**: 1 milestone post, 30-40 comments

**Month 2 Metrics**:
- Comments written: 140-175
- Posts created: 3
- Journey posts: 2
- Email signups: 20-50 (from journey posts)

---

### Month 3: Authority Building

**Goal**: Establish expertise, share tactical frameworks

**Week 9: Tactical Playbook**
- Post: "How I validated my idea before building"
- Share your framework (step-by-step)
- Include real example from your journey
- Make it actionable (others can use it)
- **Action**: 1 tactical post, 20-35 comments

**Week 10-11: Deep Engagement**
- Daily comments: 10-15/day (increasing presence)
- Answer questions in detail (not one-liners)
- Share your experience when relevant to discussions
- DM 3-5 people who mentioned similar problems
- **Action**: 140-210 comments

**Week 12: Honest Post-Mortem**
- Post: "What didn't work this month (and what I'd change)"
- Share failures transparently (builds massive trust)
- Include lessons learned
- Ask others for their failure stories
- **Action**: 1 post-mortem post, 40-60 comments

**Month 3 Metrics**:
- Comments written: 175-260
- Posts created: 3
- Authority established: Yes (people DM you for advice)
- Email list: 50-150 collected

---

### Month 4: Soft Launch

**Goal**: Validate with fake door, collect pre-orders

**Week 13: Fake Door Test**
- Post: "Based on 3 months of feedback, I built a simple version"
- Link to landing page (include pricing)
- Ask: "Would you use this? What's missing?"
- Respond immediately to all comments
- **Action**: 1 fake door post, 25-40 comments
- **Result**: 30-80 email signups, 5-15 pre-orders

**Week 14-15: Pre-Launch Momentum**
- Daily engagement: 15-20 min/day
- Share progress on build: "Launching in 2 weeks"
- Offer early access discount to community
- Create waitlist (scarcity + urgency)
- **Action**: 2 progress updates, 100-140 comments

**Week 16: Launch Week**
- Monday: "Launching Friday! Here's the final product"
- Wednesday: "2 days away - here's a sneak peek"
- Friday: **LAUNCH DAY** - "We're live! Here's what we built"
- Saturday: Post-launch update with initial numbers
- **Action**: 4 launch-week posts, respond to all comments
- **Result**: 20-50 paying customers from community

**Month 4 Metrics**:
- Comments written: 130-180
- Posts created: 7
- Pre-orders: 10-30
- Launch customers: 20-50
- **Total community-built customers: 50-100**

## Post Types and Frequency

### Weekly Post Mix (Optimal)

**Journey/Milestone Posts** (2-3/month)
- Revenue milestones
- Customer count updates
- Feature launches
- Anniversary posts
- **Engagement**: 30-50+ comments

**Tactical Playbooks** (1-2/month)
- How-to frameworks
- Step-by-step processes
- Tools and resources
- Templates and checklists
- **Engagement**: 15-25 comments

**Post-Mortems** (1/month)
- Failure analysis
- What didn't work
- Lessons learned
- Pivot stories
- **Engagement**: 40-60+ comments

**AMAs/Q&A** (1 every 2-3 months)
- When you hit milestones
- When you have expertise to share
- When community requests it
- **Engagement**: 5-15 comments

### Daily Engagement Mix

**Comments on others' posts**:
- Goal: 5-15 comments/day
- Quality: Detailed, helpful, specific
- Topics: Where you can provide genuine value

**Responding to comments on your posts**:
- Goal: Respond to 100% within 24 hours
- Best: Respond within minutes (while post is live)
- Quality: Thoughtful, grateful, engaging

### Platform-Specific Frequency

| Platform | Posts/Week | Comments/Day | Time Investment |
|----------|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Indie Hackers** | 2-3 | 5-10 | 10-15 min/day |
| **Reddit** | 1-2 per sub | 3-5 per sub | 10 min/sub/day |
| **Twitter/X** | 3-5 | 10-15 | 15 min/day |
| **LinkedIn** | 2-3 | 5-10 | 10 min/day |

## Content Repurposing Framework

### One Core Piece → 10+ Assets

**Step 1: Create Pillar Content**
- 2,000-word tactical guide on your blog
- Example: "The Complete Guide to Validating SaaS Ideas"

**Step 2: Repurpose into Social Posts**

**From blog post → Twitter threads**:
- Each H2 becomes a thread (10-15 threads total)
- Thread template: Hook → Point 1 → Point 2 → Point 3 → CTA
- Post 1 thread/day for 2 weeks

**From blog post → LinkedIn posts**:
- Each section becomes standalone post (5-7 posts)
- Longer form (300-500 words)
- Post every other day for 2 weeks

**From blog post → Indie Hackers posts**:
- Tactical post: "How I validate ideas (step-by-step)"
- Journey post: "How this guide led to my first 10 customers"
- Post-mortem: "Mistakes I made when validating"

**From blog post → Reddit posts**:
- r/SaaS: "My validation framework, tear it apart"
- Niche sub: "How to validate in [specific industry]"
- r/SideProject: "Tools I use for validation"

**Step 3: Create Visual Assets**

**From blog post → Images**:
- Canva carousel: 5-7 slides summarizing key points
- Share to LinkedIn, Twitter
- Link back to full guide

**From blog post → Short-form video**:
- Loom recording: Screen walkthrough of framework
- YouTube Shorts / TikTok: Key point as 60-second video
- Share multiple clips over weeks

**Step 4: Email Nurture Sequence**

**From blog post → 5-day email course**:
- Day 1: Welcome + link to full guide
- Day 2: Quick tip from guide
- Day 3: Common mistake + how to avoid
- Day 4: Case study example
- Day 5: Your product as ultimate solution

## Time Investment Breakdown

### Realistic Weekly Time Commitment

**Week 1-4 (Foundation)**:
- Reading/learning: 2 hrs/week
- Commenting: 5 hrs/week
- Posts: 1 hr/week
- **Total: 8 hrs/week**

**Week 5-12 (Authority Building)**:
- Commenting: 6 hrs/week
- Writing posts: 3 hrs/week
- Engaging with responses: 2 hrs/week
- **Total: 11 hrs/week**

**Week 13-16 (Launch)**:
- Commenting: 4 hrs/week
- Launch prep: 5 hrs/week
- Launch week: 15 hrs (one week spike)
- **Total: 10-12 hrs/week average**

**Month 4+ (Sustain)**:
- Commenting: 4 hrs/week
- Weekly posts: 2 hrs/week
- Engagement: 1 hr/week
- **Total: 7 hrs/week**

### Efficiency Tips

**Batch content creation**:
- Write all posts for month in one 4-hour block
- Schedule using Buffer or similar tool
- Frees up daily time for engagement

**Reuse content across platforms**:
- One core piece → 10+ derivatives
- Don't create from scratch every time
- 80% reuse, 20% fresh per platform

**Engage in focused blocks**:
- Morning: 20 minutes while coffee
- Lunch: 15 minutes between meetings
- Evening: 15 minutes before dinner
- **Total: 50 minutes/day**

### Success Benchmarks by Month

| Month | Followers | Engagement | Signups | Customers |
|-------|-----------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **1** | 50-100 | 5-10 comments/post | 0-10 | 0 |
| **2** | 200-400 | 15-25 comments/post | 20-50 | 0-5 |
| **3** | 500-1K | 25-40 comments/post | 50-150 | 0-10 |
| **4** | 1K-2K | 30-50+ comments/post | 100-300 | 20-50 |
| **6** | 2K-5K | 40-60+ comments/post | 200-500 | 50-100 |

**Red flags**:
- ❌ Consistently <5 comments per post after Month 2
- ❌ Zero DMs or inbound interest after Month 3
- ❌ Email list not growing
- ❌ Negative feedback on self-promotion
