## Proactive Check-In Email Templates

### Day 3 Check-In (Low Activity)

**Subject**: Haven't seen you lately 👋

```
Hi [Name],

I noticed you signed up for [Product] but haven't finished setup yet.

Everything okay? Sometimes people get stuck on:
- [Common obstacle 1]
- [Common obstacle 2]
- [Common obstacle 3]

Quick question: What's holding you back?

Just hit reply - I read every email personally and want to help.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
Founder, [Product]

P.S. Here's the link to complete setup if you're ready: [link]
```

### Day 7 Check-In (No Activity)

**Subject**: Quick question about your [Product] experience

```
Hi [Name],

You've been with us for a week now, and I wanted to check in:

How's [Product] working for you so far?

I'm [Your Name], founder of [Product], and I personally read every response.

Just hit reply with one of these:
👍 Loving it
🤔 Have a question
😞 Not what I expected

Your feedback helps me improve [Product] for everyone.

If you haven't had a chance to fully explore yet, no worries!
Here's a quick guide to get started: [link]

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Day 14 Inactive (At-Risk)

**Subject**: Can I help you get more value from [Product]?

```
Hi [Name],

I noticed you haven't used [Product] in a week or so.

Usually when this happens, it's because:
1. Life got busy (totally understandable!)
2. [Feature] wasn't clear how to use
3. [Product] isn't the right fit right now

Can I help?

Reply with what's going on, and I'll:
- Answer any questions
- Share how others like you are using [Product]
- Help you get unstuck

If [Product] isn't right for you, no worries - just let me know.

Either way, I'd love to hear from you.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## Exit Survey Templates

### Cancellation Email

**Subject**: Sorry to see you go 😔

```
Hi [Name],

I'm sorry to see you go, but I understand that [Product] might
not be the right fit for everyone.

I have one quick favor to ask:

**What's the primary reason for cancelling?**

Just reply with one of these:
1. Too expensive
2. Missing features
3. Found a better alternative
4. Not using it enough
5. Other (please explain)

Your feedback helps me improve [Product] for future customers.

[Optional]: If you're open to it, I'd love to hop on a 10-minute call
to understand what I could have done better. No sales pitch, I promise.

Let me know if you're interested.

Best of luck with everything,
[Your Name]
Founder, [Product]

P.S. If you change your mind, your account is still active for [X days].
You can reactivate anytime by logging in.
```

### Post-Cancellation (30 Days Later)

**Subject**: Quick question (2 min max)

```
Hi [Name],

I know you cancelled [Product] a month ago, and I'm not trying to
win you back (I promise!).

I just have one quick question:

**What's the ONE thing I could have done differently to keep you as
a customer?**

Just hit reply with your answer - it would mean a lot to me.

I'm genuinely trying to improve [Product], and your feedback would
be incredibly valuable.

Thanks in advance,
[Your Name]

P.S. No sales pitch, no trying to win you back. Just honest feedback.
```

## Email Automation Best Practices

### Timing and Frequency

**Week 1**: Daily (Days 0, 1, 3, 7) - High touch when relationship forms **Month
1**: Weekly check-ins **Month 2-3**: Bi-weekly check-ins **Month 3+**: Monthly
(unless at-risk)

### When to Send

**Best times**:

- Tuesday-Thursday: 9am-11am local time
- Avoid: Monday (catch-up mode), Friday (weekend mode)
- Avoid: Early morning or late evening

### Personalization Elements

**Include**:

- Customer's name (always)
- Company name (if B2B)
- Specific product usage data
- Relevant feature recommendations
- Founder signature (human touch)

**Avoid**:

- Generic greetings ("Dear customer")
- One-size-fits-all content
- Purely promotional emails
- No-reply email addresses

## Measuring Email Effectiveness

### Key Metrics

**Open rate**: Target >25%

- Below 15%: Subject line problem
- 15-25%: Room for improvement
- Above 25%: Excellent

**Click rate**: Target >3%

- Below 1%: Content or CTA problem
- 1-3%: Room for improvement
- Above 3%: Excellent

**Response rate**: Target >10%

- Below 5%: Content not engaging
- 5-10%: Room for improvement
- Above 10%: Excellent (founder replies are gold)

### A/B Test Ideas

**Subject lines**:

- Test: Personal vs generic
- Test: Question vs statement
- Test: Benefit vs curiosity

**CTA buttons**:

- Test: "Get started" vs "Start free trial"
- Test: "Learn more" vs "See how it works"
- Test: With vs without emoji

**Send times**:

- Test: Morning vs afternoon
- Test: Tuesday vs Thursday
- Test: With vs without "URGENT" tag
