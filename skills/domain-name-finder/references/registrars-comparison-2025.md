## Registrar Details

### Cloudflare

**Best for:** Privacy-focused users, DNS management

**Pros:**

- At-cost pricing (no markup)
- Free WHOIS privacy forever
- Excellent free DNS management
- No transfer lock (easy to move)
- Security features included

**Cons:**

- Email/support only (no phone)
- No email hosting included
- Fewer TLDs available
- No domain marketplace

**Pricing (2025):**

- .com: $10.14/year
- .io: $35.00/year
- .ai: Not available

**Best for:** Tech-savvy users, privacy advocates

### Porkbun

**Best for:** Lowest prices, simple interface

**Pros:**

- Lowest prices industry-wide
- Free WHOIS privacy
- No hidden fees
- Good UX
- Lots of TLDs

**Cons:**

- Smaller company
- Email/chat only (no phone support)
- Less brand recognition

**Pricing (2025):**

- .com: $9.13/year
- .io: $32.05/year
- .ai: $85.43/year

**Best for:** Budget-conscious buyers, personal projects

### Namecheap

**Best for:** Support balance, ease of use

**Pros:**

- Competitive pricing
- Free WHOIS privacy
- Chat and phone support
- Good UX
- Regular promotions

**Cons:**

- Upsells during checkout
- Higher renewal prices for some TLDs
- Transfer lock periods

**Pricing (2025):**

- .com: $10.98/year (first year), $13.98+ (renewal)
- .io: $34.98/year
- .ai: $88.98/year

**Best for:** Beginners, those wanting phone support

### GoDaddy

**Best for:** Premium domains, phone support

**Pros:**

- 24/7 phone support
- Largest domain marketplace
- Good for aftermarket/premium domains
- Bundled services (hosting, email)

**Cons:**

- Higher prices
- WHOIS privacy costs extra (~$10/year)
- Aggressive upsells
- Transfer lock (60-day hold)
- Interface complexity

**Pricing (2025):**

- .com: $11.99/year (first year), $17.99+ (renewal)
- WHOIS privacy: ~$10/year extra

**Best for:** Premium domains, phone support, bundled services

### Google Domains → Squarespace

**Status:** Google sold to Squarespace (Sept 2023)

**Migration:**

- Existing customers being transferred
- New registrations through Squarespace
- Pricing and features may change

**Recommendation:** Avoid for new registrations (uncertain future)

## Additional Considerations

### Transfer Fees

Most registrars offer **free transfers** (extends registration by 1 year):

- Cloudflare: Free
- Porkbun: Free
- Namecheap: Free
- GoDaddy: ~$10 fee

**Transfer process:** 5-7 days, requires unlock + auth code

### WHOIS Privacy

**Always get it** - protects personal info from public database:

- Cloudflare, Porkbun, Namecheap: Free
- GoDaddy: ~$10/year extra

### Auto-Renewal

**Enable if:** Critical domain, don't want to lose it **Disable if:** Testing
ideas, might not renew

**Set calendar reminder** 30 days before expiration

### DNS Management

**Best:** Cloudflare (included free), Porkbun (good) **Good:** Namecheap
**Adequate:** GoDaddy

**Option:** Use Cloudflare DNS regardless of registrar (free tier)

### Email Hosting

**Not included** with most registrars. Options:

- Google Workspace ($6/month)
- Microsoft 365 ($6/month)
- ProtonMail (free tier available)
- Forwarding (often free): Forward to existing email

## Registration Tips

1. **Search for promo codes** before checkout (retailmenot, honey)
2. **Buy in bulk** for multi-year discounts (2-5 years)
3. **Avoid upsells** (domain privacy, SEO listing, etc.)
4. **Check renewal prices** before buying
5. **Enable auto-renew** for critical domains
6. **Use password manager** for registrar account
7. **Enable 2FA** for security

## Premium Domains

**What they are:**

- High-value, already registered domains
- Priced individually ($500 - $100,000+)
- Often short, keyword-rich, or .com

**Where to buy:**

- **Aftermarket:** GoDaddy, Sedo, Afternic
- **Broker:** Hire domain broker (for $10k+ budgets)

**Negotiation tips:**

- Start with 30-50% of asking price
- Be willing to walk away
- Use escrow service (Escrow.com)
- Get written agreement

## Avoid

**Network Solutions** - Expensive, aggressive upsells, poor support
**Register.com** - Same parent company as Network Solutions **1&1 (IONOS)** -
Low intro prices, high renewals **Domain scams:**

- "Domain expiration" scams (fake renewal notices)
- "SEO listing" upsells (unnecessary)
- "Domain appraisal" services (use valuator.tools if needed)
