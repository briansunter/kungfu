# Community Platforms for Validation and Engagement

Use this reference to pick the right channel for pre-build validation and early
customer conversations.

## Channel Selection Principles

- Prioritize channels where your target customer describes real workflow pain.
- Treat launch aggregators as visibility events, not full validation systems.
- Prefer repeatable conversations over one-time traffic spikes.
- Keep the feedback loop short: post, discuss, revise, and repost.

## Platform Comparison

| Platform       | Intent Level | Speed of Feedback | Best Use Case                                  |
| -------------- | ------------ | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Indie Hackers  | High         | Medium            | Ongoing founder narrative and B2B validation   |
| Reddit (niche) | Medium-High  | High              | Problem discovery and objection testing        |
| Hacker News    | High         | High              | Technical products and architecture discussion |
| Product Hunt   | Event-driven | Medium            | Launch announcement and social proof           |

## Platform Playbooks

### Indie Hackers

Use for longitudinal validation.

Execution:

1. Publish weekly progress with specifics.
2. Share mistakes and design tradeoffs.
3. Ask direct questions about willingness to pay and use cases.
4. Follow up publicly on what feedback changed.

Strong signals:

- Repeat commenters from your target segment.
- Direct requests for access or demos.
- Detailed objections that help narrow scope.

### Reddit

Use for fast signal testing in niche communities.

Execution:

1. Week 1: read rules, identify active threads, capture language patterns.
2. Week 2: contribute helpful answers without links.
3. Week 3: post problem framing and request critique.
4. Week 4: post fake-door page for intent capture.

Strong signals:

- People describe current workaround costs.
- Comments compare your concept against incumbent tools.
- DMs or replies request next steps.

### Hacker News

Use for technically sophisticated feedback.

Execution:

1. Submit clear, non-hype title and context.
2. Explain architecture and decisions briefly.
3. Engage in comments with specifics.

Strong signals:

- Questions about implementation details and constraints.
- Constructive critiques that improve core assumptions.

### Product Hunt

Use as a launch milestone after validation.

Execution:

1. Prepare assets and positioning before launch day.
2. Publish a strong maker first comment.
3. Route interest into your onboarding and feedback system.
4. Publish a post-launch debrief.

Strong signals:

- Meaningful onboarding starts and demo requests, not only upvotes.

## Validation Sequence (Recommended)

1. Start with one depth channel (Indie Hackers or a high-fit subreddit).
2. Add one breadth channel for additional demand signals.
3. Run fake-door tests with real pricing and clear CTA.
4. Launch publicly once early demand and messaging are stable.

## Decision Rules

Proceed when:

- Target users repeatedly confirm the same pain.
- You receive concrete interest signals (waitlist, demos, pilot asks).
- Pricing conversations do not collapse demand quality.

Pause or pivot when:

- Feedback stays generic and non-committal.
- Only peers (not buyers) engage.
- Objections remain unresolved after multiple iterations.
