## Tier Differentiation Framework

### The 2-3 Feature Rule

Each tier needs 2-3 **must-have features** not available in the tier below.

**Example (Project Management Tool)**:

**Basic ($49/month)**

- Up to 10 projects
- 5 team members
- Basic task management
- Email support

**Pro ($99/month)** - Most Popular

- Everything in Basic, plus:
- **Unlimited projects** ← Feature 1
- **Up to 50 team members** ← Feature 2
- **Time tracking** ← Feature 3
- **Priority support** ← Feature 4
- **Advanced reporting** ← Feature 5

**Enterprise ($299/month)**

- Everything in Pro, plus:
- **Unlimited team members** ← Feature 1
- **SSO & advanced security** ← Feature 2
- **Dedicated account manager** ← Feature 3
- **Custom integrations** ← Feature 4
- **SLA guarantee** ← Feature 5

**Key principle**: Make it obvious which tier to choose based on needs

## Feature Presentation Best Practices

### Do's ✅

1. **Focus on outcomes, not inputs**
   - ✅ "Automate client reporting" (outcome)
   - ❌ "CSV export feature" (input)

2. **Use active language**
   - ✅ "Track time in one click"
   - ❌ "Time tracking functionality"

3. **Differentiate clearly**
   - ✅ Each tier has 2-3 unique features
   - ❌ Features overlap across tiers

4. **Keep lists focused**
   - ✅ 5-7 features per tier
   - ❌ 20+ features per tier (overwhelming)

### Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't use technical jargon**
   - ❌ "REST API access"
   - ✅ "Connect to 500+ apps"

2. **Don't bury the lead**
   - ❌ Most important feature #7
   - ✅ Most important feature #1

3. **Don't use vague language**
   - ❌ "Advanced features"
   - ✅ "Unlimited projects, priority support"

## Price Presentation Tactics

### Charm Pricing (End with 9)

**Examples**:

- $49/month (not $50)
- $99/month (not $100)
- $299/month (not $300)

**Why it works**: $49 feels significantly less than $50 despite $1 difference.

**Used by**: CodeSnippets Pro, Melapress, WP Activity Log

### Annual Discount Display

**Formula**: Show annual price as "X months free"

**Example**:

- Monthly: $99/month
- Annual: $990/year (save $198 - 2 months free)

**Result**: 16-20% more customers choose annual when framed as "X months free"

### Anchor High, Sell Middle

**Display order**:

1. **Pro ($99)** - Highlighted as "Most Popular"
2. Basic ($49)
3. Enterprise ($299)

**Why**: Seeing $99 first makes $49 feel reasonable. Seeing $299 makes $99 feel
like a deal.

**A/B Test Result**: 30% increase in mid-tier uptake when displayed as anchor

## FAQ Section Templates

### Common Objections + Rebuttals

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?** A: Yes, cancel anytime with no questions asked. No
penalties or fees.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my limits?** A: We'll notify you before you hit
your limit. You can upgrade to a higher tier or purchase add-ons.

**Q: Is there a free trial?** A: Yes, start a 14-day free trial. No credit card
required.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for annual billing?** A: Yes, save up to 20% when
you pay annually.

**Q: What's your refund policy?** A: 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not
satisfied, we'll refund your payment.

**Q: Can I change plans later?** A: Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. Prorated
adjustments applied automatically.

**Q: Do you offer nonprofit/education discounts?** A: Yes, contact us for
special pricing.

## Common Pricing Page Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Too Many Tiers**

- **Problem**: Decision paralysis, lower conversion
- **Solution**: Max 3-4 tiers

**Mistake 2: No Clear Differentiation**

- **Problem**: Customers can't choose between tiers
- **Solution**: 2-3 must-have features per tier not in lower tiers

**Mistake 3: Hidden Pricing**

- **Problem**: Wastes time, lowers trust
- **Solution**: Always show pricing publicly

**Mistake 4: Feature-Focused Copy**

- **Problem**: Doesn't communicate value
- **Solution**: Focus on outcomes and benefits

**Mistake 5: No Social Proof**

- **Problem**: Low credibility, low trust
- **Solution**: Customer numbers, logos, testimonials

**Mistake 6: Annual Pricing Confusion**

- **Problem**: Customers don't see value
- **Solution**: Frame as "X months free"
