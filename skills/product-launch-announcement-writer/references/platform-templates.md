# Platform-Specific Launch Templates

Complete, ready-to-use templates for launching products across different
platforms.

## Product Hunt Launch Template

```
Introducing [Product Name] - [key benefit in 5 words]

[Product Name] is a [category] that helps [target audience] [solve problem]
without [pain point].

Key features:
• [Feature 1]: [Benefit]
• [Feature 2]: [Benefit]
• [Feature 3]: [Benefit]

Perfect for [target audience] who want [desired outcome].

🎯 Try it free: [link]
💬 Ask me anything about [topic]!
```

## LinkedIn Launch Template

audience] [solve problem].

[Industry context/Problem statement]

Many [professionals/teams/companies] struggle with [pain point]. I experienced
this firsthand when [personal story].

That's why I built [Product Name].

## Key Benefits

• [Benefit 1]: [Feature that delivers it] • [Benefit 2]: [Feature that delivers
it] • [Benefit 3]: [Feature that delivers it]

## Who It's For

I'd love to hear your feedback. Have you faced [problem]? How did you solve it?

## Email Launch Template

```markdown
Subject: Introducing [Product Name] - [Key Benefit]

Hi [Name],

I'm excited to share something I've been working on: [Product Name].

[Product Name] is a [category] that helps [target audience] [solve problem].

**The Problem:** [Describe the pain point in 2-3 sentences]

**The Solution:** [Product Name] [how it solves the problem in 1 sentence]

**Key Features:** • [Feature 1] - [Benefit] • [Feature 2] - [Benefit] • [Feature
3] - [Benefit]

[Link to demo video or screenshot]

**Launch Offer:** [Special discount or offer for early adopters]

[Button: Try [Product Name] Free]

I built [Product Name] because [personal motivation]. I hope it helps you
[desired outcome].

I'd love to hear your feedback. Just reply to this email with any thoughts or
questions.

Best, [Your Name]

P.S. [Bonus insight, additional benefit, or urgency]
```
